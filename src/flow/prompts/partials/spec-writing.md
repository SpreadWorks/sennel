**Spec writing and revision guidance:**

- Preserve every condition, value, identifier, literal, valid input, expected result, and rejection condition established by the authoritative inputs. Keep technical details exact. Do not omit, weaken, broaden, or silently change them; do not invent values or behavior.
- State shared conditions once when the same condition applies to both requirements and tasks, and identify which entries they govern. State applicability, references, and differences or exceptions so each target's full conditions can be reconstructed unambiguously. Distinguish omitted or absent values from values inherited from a shared condition. Keep requirement-specific and task-specific conditions clear; do not generalize exceptions or infer values or ownership from names.
- Treat 64 KiB of canonical saved UTF-8 Spec content as authoring guidance only. It is neither a hard limit nor permission to truncate, omit, or silently alter content. Do not invent values or shorten away conditions to meet that size; retain all required content even when the resulting Spec is larger.
- Respect the current step's scope, editing authority, and immutable source input. Apply only changes the step permits, using its specified output and publication path. When implementation facts are missing, explore and read the execution checkout yourself, following the supplied checkout research guidance.
